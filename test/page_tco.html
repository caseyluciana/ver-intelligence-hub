VER TCO Advisory
Total Cost of Ownership analysis is a foundational input to every VER engagement advisory. License or contract value alone rarely reflects true cost. VER benchmarks suggest hidden costs — implementation, integration, support overhead, and exit costs — typically add 40–80% above the stated contract value for SaaS engagements and 25–60% for SOW engagements. Use this calculator to surface the full picture before any renewal, new engagement, or strategic decision.
